The work

Match the soap to the water, then keep the tunnel moving.

Most washes over-dose chemistry to hide a water profile or a tired injector. CWM’s work is dilution, reclaim, and a PM cadence that catches a failed foot valve before Saturday volume dies.

  • ChemistryPresoaks, tri-foam, wheel & tire, wax / ceramic / graphene, drying agent — quoted by pack size.
  • EquipmentSpec, install, upgrade, and repair. Full lifecycle — not a one-time drop of a conveyor.
  • MaintenanceScheduled service so a bung cap does not take the bay down at noon.
