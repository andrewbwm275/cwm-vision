Chemistry

Presoak first. Foam for the show. Protectant for the exit.

The live catalog prices 5 / 15 / 30 gallon packs where listed. This page does not invent performance percentages. Water quality and injector health decide whether a gallon actually cleans.

High pHAdvanced, intermediate, basic presoak

Second-step and one-step alkaline cleaners for tunnel and in-bay.

Low pHAdvanced, intermediate, basic

First-step / two-step acids. Basic is a non-HF acid blend.

NeutralNeutral pH presoak

General-purpose when the water and wrap do not want a hard pH swing.

Tri-foamBlue, green, red, yellow, white

Neutral tri-foam detergents for color and foam arches.

WheelsAcid and non-acid wheel cleaner

No-scrub wheel and tire. Acid SKU is quoted; non-acid is on the 5-gal card.

TiresSolvent shine and water-based shine

Gloss that lasts a trip home, not a photoshoot.

ProtectSealant, ceramic, carnauba, graphene

Exit-arch protectants. Pick by water-shed and foam, not by marketing names.

FinishDrying agent, glass cleaner

Sheeting at the blower. Ammonia-free glass for the detail finish.

HousekeepingWrap lube, wall cleaner, towel soap

Keep brushes, bays, and towels from eating the margin.
